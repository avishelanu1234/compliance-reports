# Incident Response Plan Enhancements Implementation Plan

This implementation plan outlines detailed tasks, timelines, responsibilities, and milestones for enhancing the incident response plan based on PCI-DSS guidelines and recommendations.

---

## Objectives
- Automate incident detection and response workflows.
- Conduct regular incident response drills.
- Maintain detailed forensic logs.
- Develop and maintain a comprehensive incident response plan.
- Test incident response plan annually.
- Integrate incident response with SIEM systems.
- Implement real-time threat intelligence feeds.
- Define clear roles and responsibilities.
- Establish communication protocols.
- Conduct post-incident reviews.
- Manage third-party incident reporting.
- Provide continuous training and awareness.
- Leverage automation in evidence collection and recovery.

---

## Implementation Tasks and Timeline

| Task | Description | Timeline | Responsibility | Milestone |
|------|-------------|----------|----------------|-----------|
| 1. Gap Analysis | Assess current incident response capabilities against PCI-DSS recommendations | Weeks 1-2 | Security Team Lead | Gap analysis report |
| 2. Tool Evaluation and Selection | Identify and select automation tools for detection, response, logging, and threat intelligence | Weeks 2-4 | Security Architect | Tool selection documented |
| 3. Incident Response Plan Update | Review and update incident response plan to include new workflows, roles, and procedures | Weeks 3-5 | IR Team Lead | Updated incident response plan approved |
| 4. Integration with SIEM | Connect incident response workflows and alerts with SIEM system | Weeks 4-6 | Security Engineer | SIEM integration completed |
| 5. Automation Implementation | Deploy automation for detection, evidence collection, containment, and recovery | Weeks 5-8 | Security Engineer | Automation workflows operational |
| 6. Role Assignment and Training | Define roles, assign responsibilities, and conduct role-based training | Weeks 6-9 | HR & IR Team Lead | Training completion report |
| 7. Communication Protocol Development | Develop internal and external communication plans and protocols | Weeks 7-9 | IR Team Lead & Communications | Communication plan approved |
| 8. Incident Response Drills | Schedule and conduct tabletop and live incident response exercises | Starting Week 8, ongoing quarterly | IR Team Lead | Drill reports and lessons learned |
| 9. Forensic Logging Implementation | Setup centralized logging with retention and integrity controls | Weeks 5-7 | Security Engineer | Logging system operational |
| 10. Threat Intelligence Integration | Subscribe and integrate threat feeds into SIEM and response workflows | Weeks 6-8 | Security Architect | Threat intelligence feeds active |
| 11. Post-incident Review Process | Establish formal post-incident review procedures and documentation | Weeks 8-10 | IR Team Lead | Post-incident review templates and process |
| 12. Third-party Incident Handling | Define procedures and contractual requirements for third-party incident reporting | Weeks 8-10 | Legal & IR Team Lead | Procedures documented and contracts updated |
| 13. Continuous Training Program | Develop ongoing training and awareness programs with regular updates | Starting Week 9, ongoing | HR & Security Awareness Team | Training program launched |

---

## Monitoring and Metrics
- Track completion of each task against timeline.
- Measure drill participation and effectiveness.
- Monitor incident detection and response times post-automation.
- Review post-incident reports for continuous improvement.
- Assess training completion rates and feedback.

---

## Stakeholder Engagement
- Regular updates to senior management and IT leadership.
- Coordination with legal, HR, and communications teams.
- Involve relevant business units in drills and training.

---

This plan provides a structured approach to enhancing the incident response capabilities to meet PCI-DSS compliance and improve organizational resilience.
