# IAM Compliance Audit Checklist

This checklist is based on IAM compliance recommendations to assess IAM roles and permissions for alignment with best practices and the principle of least privilege.

## 1. Principle of Least Privilege
- [ ] Are IAM roles granted only the minimum necessary permissions to perform their business functions?
- [ ] Are IAM roles and permissions regularly reviewed and unnecessary privileges removed?
- [ ] Is role-based access control (RBAC) used to assign permissions based on job responsibilities?

## 2. Regular Audits and Reviews
- [ ] Are periodic audits conducted on IAM roles and policies to verify alignment with business requirements?
- [ ] Is there a formal access certification process with quarterly reviews and attestations?
- [ ] Are access logs and user accounts reviewed regularly, especially after role changes or terminations?

## 3. Secure Access Controls
- [ ] Is multi-factor authentication (MFA) enforced for privileged accounts and administrative access?
- [ ] Are just-in-time (JIT) access controls implemented to grant temporary, time-limited permissions?
- [ ] Are strong authentication mechanisms and account lockout policies in place to prevent unauthorized access?

## 4. Policy Management and Documentation
- [ ] Is documentation of IAM policies, roles, and permissions maintained and kept up-to-date?
- [ ] Are clear roles and responsibilities defined for managing IAM policies and access controls?
- [ ] Is IAM policy management integrated within broader compliance frameworks and incident response plans?

## 5. Monitoring and Incident Response
- [ ] Is comprehensive logging and monitoring of IAM activities enabled?
- [ ] Are automated tools used to detect and alert on anomalous or unauthorized IAM activities?
- [ ] Are IAM role and permission reviews included in security incident response and compliance audits?

---

Use this checklist to regularly assess and improve your IAM compliance posture, ensuring tight access control and reduced risks of unauthorized access.
