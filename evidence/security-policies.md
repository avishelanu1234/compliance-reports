# Security Policies

## Acceptable Use Policy
- Definition of acceptable use of organizational resources.

## Data Protection Policy
- Guidelines for protecting sensitive information.
- Encrypt all sensitive data at rest and in transit using strong encryption protocols such as AES-256 and TLS 1.3.
- Classify data according to sensitivity and enforce strict access controls based on least privilege.

## Access Control Policy
- Rules for granting access to information and systems.
- Enforce multi-factor authentication (MFA) for all users and service accounts accessing sensitive systems.
- Implement adaptive access controls considering device health, user behavior, and risk context.
- Continuously validate authentication and authorization for all access requests.

## Network Segmentation and Microsegmentation Policy
- Define network segmentation strategies to isolate critical assets and minimize lateral movement.
- Enforce microsegmentation controls within trusted network zones.
- Regularly review and update segmentation policies based on threat landscape.

## Incident Response Policy
- Steps to take in the event of a security incident.
- Incorporate automated detection and adaptive response mechanisms.
- Continuously monitor audit logs and trigger alerts for anomalous activities.

## Information Security Policy
- Overview of security measures and responsibilities.
- Include zero trust principles emphasizing continuous monitoring, least privilege, and assume breach mindset.
- Provide training and awareness programs on zero trust architecture implementation.
- Establish periodic review cycles for policies and configurations to maintain alignment with evolving security best practices.
