# Incident Response Plan

## Overview
This Incident Response Plan (IRP) outlines the procedures to follow when a security incident occurs. The goal is to effectively manage and mitigate the impact of incidents.

- **Members:** [Names]

## Phases of Incident Response
1. **Preparation:** Ensure readiness to respond to incidents.
2. **Identification:** Detect and determine whether an incident has occurred.
3. **Containment:** Limit the impact of the incident.
4. **Eradication:** Remove the cause of the incident.
5. **Recovery:** Restore systems to normal operations.
6. **Lessons Learned:** Review incidents to improve future responses.
