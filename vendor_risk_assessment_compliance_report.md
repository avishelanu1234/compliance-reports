# Vendor Risk Assessment Compliance Report

## Summary
This report consolidates the compliance gaps identified in vendor risk assessment processes, emphasizing the need for a comprehensive evaluation of all third-party vendors to ensure adherence to security requirements and regulatory compliance.

## Compliance Gap
- A comprehensive vendor risk assessment has not been conducted for all third-party vendors.
- This gap exposes the organization to potential risks related to vendor security practices, data handling, and regulatory non-compliance.

## Remediation Steps
1. Identify all active vendors currently engaged by the organization.
2. Develop standardized assessment templates tailored to evaluate vendor security practices, data handling protocols, and regulatory compliance.
3. Schedule and conduct risk assessments for each identified vendor.
4. Review assessment findings thoroughly and implement necessary corrective actions based on risk levels.

## Responsible Teams
- Procurement Team
- Compliance Team
