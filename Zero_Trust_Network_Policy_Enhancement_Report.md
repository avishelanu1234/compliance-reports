# Compliance and Zero Trust Enhancements

## Overview
This document outlines the compliance aspects and recommendations for enhancing the Zero Trust Architecture posture based on the current network-policy evidence.

## Key Improvements
1. **Verification of IAM Policies**: Ensure that Identity and Access Management (IAM) policies are regularly reviewed and updated.
2. **Logging**: Implement comprehensive logging mechanisms for all access and changes to sensitive resources.
3. **Network Segmentation**: Enhance network segmentation to limit access to sensitive data.
4. **Advanced Access Controls**: Integrate advanced access controls based on user behavior and risk assessment.
5. **Automation**: Automate compliance checks and reporting processes.
6. **Continuous Risk Assessment**: Regularly assess risks associated with the Zero Trust Architecture.
7. **Secrets Management**: Implement robust secrets management practices to protect sensitive information.
8. **Policy Governance**: Establish clear governance policies for compliance and security.

## Conclusion
These enhancements aim to strengthen the compliance posture of the organization and ensure alignment with Zero Trust principles.
