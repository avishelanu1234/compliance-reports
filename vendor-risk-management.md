# Vendor Risk Management for PII Handling and Data Residency

## Overview
This document outlines the expanded vendor risk management processes to ensure compliance with PII handling and data residency requirements.

## Key Actions
1. **Periodic Security Assessments**: Conduct regular security assessments of vendors handling PII.
2. **Contract Reviews**: Ensure contracts with vendors include clauses for PII compliance and data residency.
3. **Training and Awareness**: Provide training for vendors on compliance requirements and best practices.
4. **Monitoring and Reporting**: Establish monitoring mechanisms to track vendor compliance with PII handling.

## Conclusion
These actions will enhance our vendor risk management framework and ensure compliance with data residency regulations.
