# Data-Residency Claims Tracking

This document outlines the tracking of data-residency claims and PII protection measures as part of compliance efforts. Key areas include:

- Development of data handling procedures for PII storage.
- Formalizing agreements with third-party vendors.
- Conducting Data Protection Impact Assessments (DPIAs).
- Implementing breach notification plans for PII incidents.
- Regular audits and training for compliance.

## Goals
To ensure compliance with GDPR, HIPAA, and other regulations regarding data residency and PII protection.
